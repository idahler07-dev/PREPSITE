## Prepaid plans

Paid plans are prepaid for a fixed period (for example one month, three months, or until your test day). They do not renew automatically unless you turn on automatic renewal where it is offered.

## Refunds

- If you have not used any premium features, you can ask for a full refund within **7 days** of payment.
- After that, or once premium features have been used, refunds are given at our discretion — for example if a technical problem on our side stopped you from using the service.
- Refunds go back to the same card or wallet through the original payment provider. Processing times depend on your bank.

## How to ask

Contact us using the details on the Contact page and include the email on your account and the date of payment. You can find your receipts on the Receipts page in your account.
