This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

- **Account data:** your email address, name, and — if you sign in with Telegram or Google — your Telegram ID and username or Google profile name and picture.
- **Study data:** your answers, time spent per question, scores, goals (test date, target score) and preferences (language, theme).
- **Payment data:** order amounts, dates and the payment provider's transaction ID. We never receive or store card numbers.
- **Technical data:** basic server logs (IP address, browser type) kept for security for a limited time.

## Why we use it

To run your account, show your progress, build study plans, send the emails and Telegram messages you choose, process payments, prevent abuse, and improve our questions (using anonymous, aggregated results).

## AI features

When you ask for an AI explanation, the question and your answer are sent to our AI provider to write the explanation. We do not send your name or email.

## Who we share it with

Only service providers who help us run the site (hosting, email delivery, payment processing, AI explanations), under contracts that limit their use of the data. We do not sell your data.

## Cookies

We use only essential cookies: to keep you signed in and to remember your language and theme. We do not use advertising cookies.

## Your rights

You can download your practice data and delete your account at any time from **Settings**. Deleting your account permanently removes your practice history. You can also contact us to access or correct your data.

## Contact

Use the details on our Contact page for any privacy question.
